## Research and build use

The user cannot provide or arrange a live eight-person recording. This public
record supplies real meeting data for long transcript, speaker navigation,
search, and playback tests in the rebuild.

It does not establish how Fathom performs in a live eight-person meeting.
That product check remains unverified and must be reported as a research limit.
The short Fathom tests and their findings remain the firsthand product evidence.

Use this source with attribution in the public demonstration. Identify the
recording as public research data. Keep any generated summary separate from
the source transcript. Do not present a generated summary as a Fathom result
unless it was actually obtained from Fathom.
